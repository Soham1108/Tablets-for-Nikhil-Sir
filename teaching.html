A practical recording workflow

1

Open the notes app

Prepare the lesson in Samsung Notes, Goodnotes or another handwriting app.

2

Connect a microphone

Use a good USB-C or wireless microphone for clearer explanations.

3

Record the lesson

Test screen recording with microphone audio before recording a full class.

4

Edit and upload

Trim pauses, check audio levels, export at a suitable resolution and upload to YouTube.

Before buying: Test simultaneous Zoom screen sharing and local screen recording on the exact device and app versions. Some apps or operating-system modes can restrict simultaneous capture or audio routing. A separate laptop can run Zoom while the tablet is used as the writing board.
