Section 04

The final verdict

Four rankings, each based on a different buying priority.

Best value for money

1. Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE+

Large display, included S Pen and useful teaching features at a much more sensible price.

Best all-rounder

1. iPad Air 13-inch (M4)

Excellent performance, strong creative apps and a large display. Remember to budget for the Pencil.

Best for the teaching use cases

1. Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra

Huge writing canvas, S Pen, multitasking and a premium screen. Best if budget is not a concern.

Best long-term option

1. iPad Air 13-inch (M4)

Strong performance headroom and a mature software ecosystem. Samsung's flagship update policy is also worth considering.

My recommendation

Recommended purchase

Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE+

For a maths teacher, it hits the sweet spot: a large 13.1-inch writing area, S Pen included, Samsung Notes, multitasking and a much lower overall cost than a flagship tablet.

Choose the Tab S12 Ultra if the teacher wants the most premium setup and will use it heavily for recording and content creation.

Choose the iPad Air 13 M4 if the teacher prefers iPadOS and is comfortable buying the Apple Pencil separately.

Final buying check: Confirm the exact model name, storage, Wi-Fi/5G version, stylus included in the box, current price, warranty and return policy. Treat the rankings as a practical shortlist rather than an objective benchmark.
