Section 01

Tablet display & prices

A closer look at the display, writing experience, performance and manufacturer-promoted features of every tablet in the shortlist.

Price note: Prices below are indicative India prices, not guaranteed live checkout prices. Apple prices are starting prices where available; Samsung prices can change substantially with storage, Wi-Fi/5G, bank offers and stock. Check the linked official product page before buying.
Budget Apple option

1. Apple iPad 11 (A16)

From ₹49,900
  • Display: 11-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCD, 2360 × 1640 resolution, 264 ppi, True Tone and up to 500 nits brightness.
  • Performance: A16 chip with a 5-core CPU, 4-core GPU and 16-core Neural Engine; suitable for note-taking, video calls, presentations and everyday editing.
  • Writing: Supports Apple Pencil (USB-C) and Apple Pencil (1st generation). The 1st-gen Pencil needs a USB-C to Pencil Adapter for pairing and charging.
  • Cameras & calls: 12MP landscape Center Stage front camera, 12MP rear camera, landscape stereo speakers and dual microphones.
  • Battery & connection: Up to 10 hours of Wi-Fi web/video use; USB-C, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3. Cellular models support 5G.
Apple's highlighted features
  • “Now with the superfast A16 chip” and 128GB starting storage.
  • 11-inch Liquid Retina display and all-screen design.
  • Apple Pencil support, Smart Script handwriting and Math Notes in iPadOS.
  • 12MP Center Stage landscape camera for video calls.

Teaching take: A polished, portable option for handwritten explanations and online classes. Its 11-inch workspace is usable, but it gives less room for a full-page derivation than the 13-inch tablets.

Accessory cost: Apple Pencil (USB-C) is sold separately at ₹7,900. The Pencil 1st generation is also compatible, but check adapter and pricing separately.

Premium large-screen option

2. Apple iPad Air 13-inch (M4)

From ₹84,900
  • Display: 13-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCD, 2732 × 2048 resolution, P3 wide colour, True Tone, anti-reflective coating and up to 600 nits brightness.
  • Performance: Apple M4 with an 8-core CPU, 9-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine and 12GB unified memory. Excellent headroom for multitasking and creative apps.
  • Writing: Supports Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil (USB-C), including Pencil hover. Pencil Pro adds pressure/gesture features for more advanced creative work.
  • Camera & audio: 12MP rear camera, 12MP landscape Center Stage front camera, stereo speakers and dual microphones.
  • Battery & connection: Up to 10 hours of Wi-Fi web/video use; USB-C, Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4. Cellular configuration is also available.
Apple's highlighted features
  • M4 performance in a thin and light design.
  • 13-inch Liquid Retina display with P3 wide colour and anti-reflective coating.
  • Apple Intelligence support.
  • Apple Pencil Pro support, Pencil hover and Magic Keyboard compatibility for iPad Air.

Teaching take: The bigger canvas is genuinely useful for teaching mathematics: more room for diagrams, long calculations, a PDF and a notes app side by side. It is the strongest Apple pick here if screen space matters more than portability.

Accessory cost: Apple Pencil (USB-C) ₹7,900 or Apple Pencil Pro ₹11,900; both sold separately.

Portable premium option

3. Apple iPad Air 11-inch (M4)

From ₹64,900
  • Display: 11-inch Liquid Retina IPS LCD, 2360 × 1640 resolution, P3 wide colour, True Tone, fully laminated glass, anti-reflective coating and 500 nits brightness.
  • Performance: Same M4 platform as the 13-inch Air: 8-core CPU, 9-core GPU, 16-core Neural Engine and 12GB unified memory.
  • Writing: Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil (USB-C) support, with Pencil hover. Both styluses are optional purchases.
  • Camera & audio: 12MP rear camera, landscape 12MP Center Stage front camera, stereo speakers and dual microphones.
  • Battery & connection: Up to 10 hours of Wi-Fi web/video use; USB-C, Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4.
Apple's highlighted features
  • M4 chip and Apple Intelligence.
  • Liquid Retina display with P3 wide colour, True Tone and anti-reflective coating.
  • Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil (USB-C) compatibility.
  • Magic Keyboard for iPad Air support.

Teaching take: It has the same core speed and Pencil features as the 13-inch Air in a lighter, easier-to-hold body. Choose it if you move around often; choose the 13-inch model if you mostly teach at a desk.

Accessory cost: Apple Pencil (USB-C) ₹7,900 or Apple Pencil Pro ₹11,900; sold separately.

Budget Samsung option

4. Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Lite

Around ₹32,000–₹42,000
  • Display: 10.9-inch-class (27.7cm) TFT LCD, 2112 × 1320 resolution and 90Hz refresh rate; SGS-certified low-blue-light display.
  • Performance & storage: Octa-core processor, configurations up to 8GB RAM and 256GB storage, plus microSD expansion up to 2TB.
  • Writing: S Pen support and Samsung Notes tools for handwritten notes. Samsung's Handwriting Help can straighten and tidy handwriting; Solve Math can calculate handwritten equations.
  • Battery: 8,000mAh battery with up to 16 hours of video playback (manufacturer estimate).
  • Other details: 8MP rear camera, 5MP front camera, USB-C and Bluetooth 5.3. S Pen is included in the retail box where specified by Samsung.
Samsung's highlighted features
  • 27.7cm display with 90Hz refresh rate.
  • S Pen handwriting support and Handwriting Help.
  • Solve Math in Samsung Notes for handwritten equations.
  • Expandable storage up to 2TB and compatibility with Galaxy accessories.

Teaching take: The included S Pen and Samsung's handwriting tools make this a practical entry-level teaching tablet. Its smaller display and more modest processor make it better for notes and basic classes than heavy multitasking.

Value-focused large screen

5. Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE+

Around ₹62,000–₹83,000
  • Display: 13.1-inch LCD, 2880 × 1800 resolution and 90Hz refresh rate, giving plenty of room for writing and split-screen work.
  • Performance & storage: Exynos 1580 processor; 8GB/128GB or 12GB/256GB configurations, with microSD expansion up to 1.5TB.
  • Writing: S Pen is included. Samsung Notes supports handwriting, drawing and annotation; Circle to Search and other Galaxy AI tools add convenience.
  • Battery & durability: 10,090mAh battery, 45W charging support and IP68-rated tablet/S Pen water and dust resistance.
  • Other details: 13MP rear camera, 12MP ultra-wide front camera, dual speakers and Wi-Fi 6/5G variants.
Samsung's highlighted features
  • Largest display yet in the Tab S10 FE family: 13.1 inches.
  • Large-screen multitasking and S Pen in the box.
  • Circle to Search with Google and Samsung's AI-assisted note tools.
  • IP68 protection and expandable storage.

Teaching take: A strong value proposition for a teacher: large writing area, included pen, microSD expansion and water resistance. The LCD panel is not as contrast-rich as the AMOLED models, but the screen size is excellent for board-style lessons.

Previous-generation option

6. Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE+

Around ₹40,000–₹55,000
  • Display: 12.4-inch TFT LCD, 2560 × 1600 WQXGA resolution and 90Hz refresh rate.
  • Performance & storage: Exynos 1380 processor; commonly sold in 8GB/128GB and 12GB/256GB variants, with microSD expansion up to 1TB.
  • Writing: Water- and dust-resistant S Pen included, magnetically attaches to the tablet; supports S Pen Creator Edition (sold separately).
  • Battery & durability: 10,090mAh battery and IP68-rated tablet/S Pen protection.
  • Other details: Dual rear cameras (8MP + 8MP), 12MP ultra-wide front camera and dual speakers.
Samsung's highlighted features
  • Large, vivid 12.4-inch display.
  • Redesigned S Pen included for writing, sketching and drawing.
  • IP68 water and dust resistance for the tablet and in-box S Pen.
  • Samsung Notes and multitasking features for productivity.

Teaching take: Still a capable large-screen note-taking tablet if found at a good discount. It is an older generation, so compare its price carefully against the newer S10 FE+ before choosing it.

Premium Samsung option

7. Samsung Galaxy Tab S10+

Around ₹85,000–₹1,00,000
  • Display: 12.4-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 2800 × 1752 resolution and 120Hz refresh rate, with deep blacks and vivid contrast.
  • Performance & storage: MediaTek Dimensity 9300+ flagship processor; 12GB RAM and 256GB/512GB storage options, expandable by microSD.
  • Writing: S Pen included, with Samsung Notes and AI-assisted note features. S Pen is useful for precise annotation and board-style writing.
  • Battery & durability: 10,090mAh battery, 45W charging support and IP68 water/dust resistance.
  • Other details: Quad speakers, 13MP + 8MP rear cameras, 12MP ultra-wide front camera and Samsung DeX desktop-style multitasking.
Samsung's highlighted features
  • Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with anti-reflection technology.
  • Galaxy AI tools, including Note Assist and Circle to Search.
  • In-box S Pen and Samsung Notes.
  • Samsung DeX for a desktop-like productivity layout.

Teaching take: The best all-round premium Samsung option in this list: a high-quality 120Hz AMOLED screen, included S Pen, strong performance and DeX. The 12.4-inch size is a useful middle ground between portability and writing space.

Large-screen flagship

8. Samsung Galaxy Tab S12 Ultra

From ₹1,49,999
  • Display: 14.6-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X, 2960 × 1848 resolution and 120Hz refresh rate, with peak brightness up to 1600 nits.
  • Performance & storage: MediaTek Dimensity 9500 3nm processor; 12GB RAM with 256GB/512GB storage options, plus microSD expansion up to 2TB.
  • Writing: S Pen included. The large canvas is ideal for long derivations, diagrams, annotating PDFs and keeping multiple teaching apps visible.
  • Battery & durability: 11,600mAh battery, 45W charging support, up to 23 hours of video playback (manufacturer estimate) and IP68 protection.
  • Other details: Quad speakers with spatial audio, 13MP + 8MP ultra-wide rear cameras, 12MP ultra-wide front camera and Wi-Fi 7/5G options.
Samsung's highlighted features
  • Samsung's largest tablet display, at 14.6 inches.
  • Thin 5.1mm design, in-box S Pen and up to 23-hour video playback claim.
  • IP68-rated protection and Galaxy AI productivity features.
  • Three-section Multi Window, Note Assist and support for creative apps such as Adobe Photoshop.

Teaching take: The most spacious digital-board experience here, especially for split-screen teaching and content creation. It is also the heaviest and most expensive option, so it makes sense mainly when the biggest screen is a priority.
