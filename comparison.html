Compare the tablets

The key differences that matter for teaching and recording.

Apple iPad range

Strong app ecosystem, polished note-taking apps and excellent performance. Apple Pencil is an additional purchase.

Samsung FE range

Good value, S Pen included, expandable storage on supported models and practical split-screen tools.

Samsung flagship range

Premium AMOLED screens, S Pen included, strong multitasking and Samsung DeX on supported models.
